Let It Shine

Two lights, gold and sky, take turns against the darkness, which places after every round.

Your bursts landing on your ally's lamps add +1 without taking them, so you can set off each other's chains. Wipe out the darkness, or hold more than half the board when dawn breaks after round 20. If darkness covers 75%, it wins.

Players
