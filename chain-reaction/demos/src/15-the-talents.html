The Talents

Each turn you earn talents: 1 for every 3 squares you own. Buy cards from the shop, invest talents on one of your squares (they grow +1 every turn, but whoever takes the square takes the coins), or bury them safely beside your name.

After 15 rounds the Master returns. Score = squares + talents, and invested talents count double.
