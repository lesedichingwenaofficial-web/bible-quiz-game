Turn the Other Cheek

Classic Chain Reaction for 3–6 lambs sitting in a ring. Each turn, place a sheep and play up to one card. Then draw back up to 3.

Skip the next lamb, reverse the turn order, take another turn, or choose where the next lamb's 2 sheep go. Hit with Two by Two? Play your own to pass it on with 2 more!

Players
3
