Chain Burst

Fill cells. Set off explosions. Take the board.

Players
2
    Board
    How to play
    • On your turn, tap an empty cell or one of your own to add an orb.
    • A cell explodes when it is full: corners hold 2, edges 3, the middle 4.
    • An explosion throws one orb into each neighbour and turns it your colour, so explosions can set off more explosions.
    • Once everyone has played, anyone with no orbs left is out. Last colour standing wins.
