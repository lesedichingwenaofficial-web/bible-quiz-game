Privacy, in plain words

Lamb Stampede is a free game. There are no adverts and nothing to buy.

No tracking

There are no adverts, no trackers and no cookies. We don't know who you are, and we don't want to.

Counting visits

We count how many times the game is opened, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you.

Your progress stays with you

Your wool, outfits, scores and settings are saved on your device only. They never leave it unless you copy a backup code yourself.

Email only if you choose

If you join the Flock, your email address is used only to send game news and updates. We never sell or share it. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email. The game itself only remembers that you signed up, never your address.

Children

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian needs to sign up for you.

Questions

Write to us at hello@example.com.
